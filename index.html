Questions

Does LullCam record or store video of my baby?

No. LullCam never records or stores video, audio or photos. Everything is live and nothing is written to disk.

Do I need an account?

No. LullCam has no accounts. Your family's devices pair by scanning a QR code in person.

Does it work away from home?

Yes, if you allow it. Over the internet, video and audio are end-to-end encrypted; the connection server can't see them and doesn't store them. On the same Wi‑Fi everything stays local.

What do I need?

Two Apple devices: an old iPhone or iPad acts as the camera in the nursery and your iPhone is the parent screen. No camera to buy.

How much does it cost?

Downloading to join a family is free. To create your family you need LullCam Pro: €19.99 per year with 3 days free, or €49.99 lifetime. Your partner and guests use it for free.

Will it alert me if my baby cries while my phone is locked?

Yes. LullCam alerts you about crying, noise, movement, a covered or moved camera, battery and lost connection, even with the screen locked.

Is it a medical device?

No. LullCam is not a medical device and does not replace adult supervision.
